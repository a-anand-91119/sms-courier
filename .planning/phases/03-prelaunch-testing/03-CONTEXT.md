# Phase 3: Pre-Launch Testing - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-01-15
**Status:** Ready for planning

<vision>
## How This Should Work

Dogfooding the app daily while waiting for the 14-day beta requirement to pass. The app is already live in closed testing with approval — now it's about using it in real conditions and fixing whatever surfaces.

This isn't about recruiting testers or gathering formal feedback. It's personal use: pair two devices, forward SMS in real scenarios, and experience the app as an actual user would. Issues get fixed as they come up.

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

- **Core reliability** — SMS forwarding works flawlessly every time. No missed messages.
- **UX polish** — The app feels smooth and intuitive during daily use. Rough edges get sanded down.

Both matter equally for launch readiness.

</essential>

<boundaries>
## What's Out of Scope

- New features — no feature work, only fix what's broken or rough
- Major refactors — no architecture changes, stability over improvement
- Focus purely on fixing issues, nothing else

</boundaries>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

No specific requirements — open to whatever surfaces during daily use.

</specifics>

<notes>
## Additional Context

- App already live in internal testing
- Closed (alpha) testing approved and live
- Waiting for open beta: requires 12 testers + 14 days
- Phases 1 (Play Store Submission) and 2 (CI/CD Pipeline) already complete

</notes>
